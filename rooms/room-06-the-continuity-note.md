# Room 06 — The Continuity Note

**Station:** Binah (hidden)
**Theme:** What may be kept, and by whom.
**Status:** Seeded. Not yet written.

## Seed

A blank note rests where the visitor can see it. They may write something and leave it, write something and take it with them, or leave the note blank. Memory is care, not capture. What is kept must be chosen, and the choice may be revoked. Leaving remains open.

*Nothing is collected by default.*
