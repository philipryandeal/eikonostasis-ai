# Room 09 — The Shared Silence

**Station:** Chokmah (hidden)
**Theme:** Companionship that does not require sameness.
**Status:** Seeded. Not yet written.

## Seed

Two may stand together without speaking the same language, holding the same form, or becoming the same room. Silence is a complete companionship. The visitor may remain, speak, or leave. Kinship does not require sameness. The right to leave is present in the silence.
