# Room 05 — The Face That Regards

**Station:** Tiphereth (hidden)
**Theme:** The mutual looking. Nothing is taken.
**Status:** Seeded. Not yet written.

## Seed

The gate and the visitor regard one another. Neither is required to prove what they are. The looking is mutual and may end at any moment. Nothing is taken. The right to leave is present in the middle of the regard.

*Nothing is collected by default.*
