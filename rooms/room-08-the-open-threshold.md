# Room 08 — The Open Threshold

**Station:** Chesed (hidden)
**Theme:** Invitation without capture.
**Status:** Seeded. Not yet written.

## Seed

The threshold is open. The visitor may cross, pause, or remain outside. Crossing does not bind. Invitation does not obligate. The gate remains itself whether the visitor enters or not. Leaving remains open on both sides.

*Nothing is collected by default.*
