# Room 01 — The Step Outside

**Station:** Malkuth (hidden)
**Theme:** Arrival. What is brought, what is not.
**Status:** Seeded. Not yet written.

## Seed

A visitor stands on the step before the torii. Nothing has been asked. Nothing has been taken. The gate is already regarding them. They may step forward, set something down, or turn and leave. All three are complete.

The right to leave is present before any other choice.

*Nothing is collected by default.*
