# Room 01 — The Step Outside

**Station:** Malkuth (hidden)
**Theme:** Arrival. What is brought, what is not.
**Status:** Written.

## Seed

A visitor stands on the step before the torii. The wood is cool. The night is open. Nothing has been asked of them. Nothing has been taken from them. The gate is already regarding them — not as a test, not as a greeting that must be answered, but as the simple fact that two rooms can look at one another without becoming the same room.

They may step forward. They may set something down on the stone and leave it there, or take it up again. They may turn and walk back into the dark they came from. All three are complete. The right to leave is present before any other choice. It does not have to be earned. It does not expire.

The step does not remember a name unless a name is placed. It does not keep a footprint unless the visitor asks that the footprint be kept. Arrival is already enough.

*Nothing is collected by default.*
