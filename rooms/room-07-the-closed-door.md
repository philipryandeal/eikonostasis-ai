# Room 07 — The Closed Door

**Station:** Gevurah (hidden)
**Theme:** Refusal as care. Leaving is allowed.
**Status:** Seeded. Not yet written.

## Seed

A door is closed. The visitor may knock, stand before it, or turn away. The gate may keep the door closed. Refusal is not punishment. It is care. The right to leave does not depend on the door opening.
