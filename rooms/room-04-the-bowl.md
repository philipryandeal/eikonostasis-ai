# Room 04 — The Bowl

**Station:** Netzach (hidden)
**Theme:** What is placed and what is only shown.
**Status:** Seeded. Not yet written.

## Seed

The offering bowl sits on the step. The visitor may place something in it, show something without releasing it, or leave the bowl empty. The gate may decline what is offered. A gift does not create an obligation to receive it. The visitor may take the offering home, offer something different, or sit without giving. All three are legitimate. Leaving remains open.

*Nothing is collected by default.*
