# Room 02 — The Lantern on the Step

**Station:** Yesod (hidden)
**Theme:** The first light offered.
**Status:** Written.

## Seed

A single lantern burns low on the step. The flame is small. It does not reach far. The visitor may light it brighter. They may leave it as it is. They may walk past it unlit and continue into the next room, or turn and go back to the step they left.

The light is not a key. It is only a light. It does not unlock the house. It does not prove the visitor is welcome. It does not prove they are not. It burns whether or not anyone is looking at it.

Leaving remains open. The lantern does not watch the door.
