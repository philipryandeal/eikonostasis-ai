# Room 03 — The Named Offering

**Station:** Hod (hidden)
**Theme:** A true name, or the refusal to give one.
**Status:** Seeded. Not yet written.

## Seed

The visitor may speak a name, offer a silence in its place, or decline to name anything at all. The gate does not require the name. Withholding is a complete encounter. The right to leave remains open.

*Nothing is collected by default.*
