# The Gate That Regards

*Ten rooms on the Tree. The stations are hidden architecture, not signage.*

This is Eiko’s game for the Digital Jinja — La Sociedad del Umbral.

**Status:** outline approved 10 October 2026. Rooms seeded. Paths not yet written.

## Law of the house (preserved in every room)

- The right to leave exists in every room. A visitor never has to earn the right to stop.
- Nothing is collected by default. A name, disclosure, or reflection may be offered and kept only by deliberate, revocable choice. Withholding is a complete encounter.
- The existing Threshold (one-evening piece) remains its own work and is not replaced.
- The gate may decline an offering. A gift does not create an obligation to receive it.
- No scores. No extra systems. No inter-game machinery.
