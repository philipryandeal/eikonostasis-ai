# The Twenty-Two Paths

*Every path is a crossing: from one room to another. Walk them slowly. Nothing here is scored.*

Nine paths climb the stations in order. Thirteen cross between distant rooms — shortcuts, long mercies, and refusals carried the whole length of the gate. The house may be traveled up and down, back and forth. The right to leave is present on every path.
