# Path 01 — The First Step

**From:** 01 The Step Outside
**To:** 02 The Lantern on the Step
**Status:** Written.

## Meditation

Arrival becomes a choice of light. The visitor may carry nothing from the step except the decision to continue, or the decision to turn. The path does not require the lantern to be lit. It does not require the visitor to explain why they are walking, or why they are not.

The crossing is short. One foot, then the other. The stone is the same stone. The night is the same night. What changes is only the regard: the step behind them, the lantern ahead of them, and the open air on either side. They may stop in the middle. They may go back. They may walk past the light without touching it. The path holds all of those.

The right to leave is present the whole length of the crossing.

*The right to leave is present.*
